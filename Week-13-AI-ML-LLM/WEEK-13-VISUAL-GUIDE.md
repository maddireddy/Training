### Evolution of AI Models

```
Timeline of AI Progress:

1950s-1980s: Rule-Based AI
  └─► Expert systems, symbolic AI

1990s-2000s: Statistical ML
  ├─► Linear models
  ├─► Decision trees
  └─► SVM, naive Bayes

2010s: Deep Learning Era
  ├─► CNNs (ImageNet 2012)
  ├─► RNNs, LSTMs
  └─► Word2Vec, GloVe

2017+: Transformer Revolution
  ├─► Attention is All You Need (2017)
  ├─► BERT (2018)
  ├─► GPT-2, GPT-3 (2019-2020)
  └─► ChatGPT, GPT-4 (2022-2023)

2024+: Multimodal & AGI Research
  └─► Vision + Language models
```
